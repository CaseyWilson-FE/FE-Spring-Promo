Questions

What counts as an "event" for this offer?

A staffed Food Envy event of 30 guests or more, with our team on site. This is different from delivered (drop-off) catering, which isn't eligible for this offer, along with any booking under 30 guests.

Is there a minimum spend?

No, beyond the 30-guest requirement.

When do I actually get the Glou Glou gift card?

Once your booking deposit is confirmed.

How long is the gift card valid for?

12 months from the date it's issued.

What can I spend it on?

Anything at Glou Glou: coffee and pastries, brunch, lunch, or the wine bar in the evening.

What if I need to cancel my event?

$200 will be deducted from your deposit refund.

Does this apply to weddings?

Yes, the offer applies to any qualifying Food Envy event, including weddings, booked for September, October or November 2026.
